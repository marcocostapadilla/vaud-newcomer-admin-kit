Campus food (EPFL restaurants, self-services, food trucks)

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Companion to CAMIPRO & matriculation. Menu prices change daily; use Offre du jour for the figure that applies to your status.

What this page is for

Where to eat on the EPFL campus, how CAMIPRO payment and status discounts work, and how to top up your card. Prefer the live menu board over any remembered CHF.

Short version

  • EPFL lists more than 30 catering outlets: restaurants with service, self-services, cafeterias, and a Food-trucks Village (hub page: Mon–Fri 11:30–14:00; evening/weekend hours noted “from April 2023”).
  • Pay by tapping your CAMIPRO on the reader; EPFL says you get discounts depending on your status.
  • Daily specials and status prices: Offre du jour.
  • Top up before lunch rush (machines, online TWINT / cards, or AGEPoly shop cash for students).

Sources: Restaurants hub · CAMIPRO payment

Your CAMIPRO status drives which column you are charged. EPFL does not publish a full prose legend beyond these tooltips on the menu page; if a till price looks wrong, check your card status strip / myCAMIPRO and the receipt.

Paying with CAMIPRO

  1. Load money onto your CAMIPRO purse (see below).
  2. At the till, place the card on the white CAMIPRO reader; the account is debited immediately.
  3. Ask for a receipt, or check the display / myCAMIPRO / EPFL Campus app for the remaining credit.

Student menu discount note (CAMIPRO FAQ): the CHF 8.- menu and the CHF 2.- discount are granted only if you pay by CAMIPRO and have enough credit on the card. Paying cash while only showing the card does not unlock that discount.

Vending machines on campus accept cash and CAMIPRO. If a CAMIPRO vend fails to deliver, EPFL says to reposition the badge so the transaction is not validated; if a product falls into a locked compartment, a second purchase may be needed to open it.

You can also eat at UNIL outlets; CAMIPRO payment page links a separate UNIL info page for that.
