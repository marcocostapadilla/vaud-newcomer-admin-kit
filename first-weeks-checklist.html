Later / only if it applies

Lost your passport or permit card? Police declaration first, then your embassy (passport) or commune (permit duplicate). → Lost passport, ID or permit card

Shipping your belongings? Furniture, boxes or a car you have used for six months can come in duty free with customs form 18.44 and an inventory. → Customs: moving your belongings

Install Alertswiss, add Vaud: the official app for sirens and public alerts. → Alertswiss, sirens and public alerts

Starting in February 2027? The same steps apply. Spring classes start on 22 Feb 2027, and the FRAC and course registration are due on 5 Mar 2027. → Semester dates
