Campus Wi-Fi: epfl vs eduroam

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Companion to CAMIPRO.

Short version

On the EPFL campus, prefer the epfl Wi-Fi network with your EPFL account (username@epfl.ch). Use eduroam when you visit other Eduroam partner campuses (for example regular trips to UNIL) and want one login everywhere.

At home? Campus Wi-Fi does not follow you there: see Home internet for a private flat.

Sources: EPFL Members - which network · Access to epfl and eduroam · WiFi hub

Which SSID when

SSID When EPFL says to use it
epflPrefer on the EPFL campus. After you install the profile, devices reconnect automatically; access includes campus intranet resources.
eduroamOnly if you travel often between Eduroam partner sites and want the same login everywhere (EPFL example: regular trips EPFL ↔ UNIL). You keep using your own institution's identifier and password (for EPFL students: username@epfl.ch).

Visitors and guests have separate Wi-Fi options on the WiFi hub; this page is for EPFL members (students with campus accounts).

Technical pointers (from EPFL access page)

General settings EPFL publishes for both SSIDs (SSID row: epfl "choose as a priority on the EPFL campus", eduroam at other universities):

  • Encryption: AES-CCMP
  • Auth: WPA2-802.1X / WPA3-802.1X (Enterprise)
  • EAP: PEAP + MS-CHAPv2 or EAP-TTLS + MS-CHAPv2
  • CA: DigiCert_Global_Root_G2.pem
  • Server: radius.epfl.ch
  • Outer identity: anonymous@epfl.ch
  • Username: Username@epfl.ch (EPFL password; the page links "Password management")
  • Domain: none ("-" on the EPFL table)

Use EPFL's profile (Apple devices) or EPFL's PDF guide (Windows, Linux, Android) rather than guessing settings by hand.

Account / 2FA

Wi-Fi logs in with your EPFL account (username@epfl.ch + EPFL password). The access page's "Password management" link points to EPFL IT's Entra ID authentication pages (Manage passwords); on 5 Oct 2026 that page showed "VPN error" from outside EPFL, so open it on campus wired / VPN or ask the IT Service Desk. The Wi-Fi pages say nothing about 2FA / MFA for Wi-Fi; this kit does not add steps EPFL does not publish. If login fails, check the password first, then reinstall the profile or re-follow the PDF guide.
