Getting around (transport)

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Day-one need: plan how you reach EPFL and buy tickets/passes from official sources. No prices here, they change; check the linked sites.

  • Swiss Federal Railways (SBB / CFF): national trains and the main ticket app/website, sbb.ch (French: cff.ch).
  • Lausanne region public transport (tl): metro m1, buses, tickets and the tl app, t-l.ch. EPFL notes the campus is served by m1 and tl buses (e.g. lines 1 and 31); confirm current routes on tl.
  • EPFL mobility (official campus guidance): how the campus connects to Renens / Lausanne / Morges, the tl sales desk at EPFL’s Maison de la mobilité, and commuting tips, EPFL, Public transportation · EPFL, Getting to the campuses.
  • Campus bike-sharing (Lime, not PubliBike): The PubliBike Lausanne-Morges / CAMIPRO "Campus" pass ended at the end of December 2025. The current network on the EPFL-UNIL campuses and across Lausanne-Morges is Lime (electric bikes). Leftover Campus CHF prices on the CAMIPRO page are historical (the banner may still read in the future tense).
    • Sign up in the Lime app with your EPFL email for the EPFL community rate: 30% off Lime per-minute pricing.
    • Mobility page amounts (checked 5 Oct 2026): EPFL community unlock CHF 0.35, about CHF 0.17/min after the 30% discount. Lime Pass (standard, no EPFL subsidy): 30 min / 24h CHF 4.95; 200 min / 7 days CHF 29.95; 400 min / 30 days CHF 53.95; 800 min / 90 days CHF 99.95. Confirm live in the Lime app before you ride.
    • EPFL Mobility: the local Lime fleet is 100% electric; 11 Lime stations on the EPFL campus (map on the Mobility page); end rides only in authorised parking zones and confirm with a photo in the app. A Lime account works on all Lime networks in Switzerland.
    • Details / map / how to ride: EPFL Bike sharing (Mobility)
    • Shutdown notice (historical Campus pass): EPFL Bike-sharing (CAMIPRO)
  • Mobility car sharing (occasional car trips): EPFL lists Mobility stations on campus at Esplanade, Rivier, Quartier Nord and EPFL Innovation Park (24/7; open with the Mobility app or a SwissPass card). Cars are station-based: return them where the reservation started.
    • Registering through the EPFL mobilityPLUS link waives the CHF 39.90 activation fee; under 28 there is no monthly fee (checked 5 Oct 2026). Since 1 March 2024 the CAMIPRO card can no longer be activated as a Mobility card.
    • Details, licence rules and deductibles: Mobility car sharing · EPFL, Carpooling, car-sharing and rental cars
  • Demand-responsive transport (DRT / tl TAD): EPFL states a DRT service is available from 17 August 2026 in the Renens and Ecublens areas and in parts of Chavannes-près-Renens, Crissier, and Saint-Sulpice. You book in the tl TAD app (iOS and Google Play); a minibus picks you up at a nearby DRT stop and takes you to your destination or to the rest of the tl / SBB network.
    • Fares (EPFL): a ticket or pass valid for zones 11-12; short-distance tickets are not valid. Ecublens: no fare supplement beyond usual Mobilis titles.
    • Hours (EPFL + Ecublens): Mon-Fri 6:00-20:00, Sat 7:00-19:00.
    • Booking help phone (EPFL + Ecublens): 021 621 08 14 (if you have difficulty booking in the app).
    • Network map (EPFL Mobility PDF, valid from 17.08.2026): zones 951 Renens and 953 Ecublens on Plan-TAD 951-953 (PDF).
    • App (linked from EPFL): tl TAD on the App Store · tl TAD on Google Play.
    • Commune summary: Ecublens news - TAD (same start date, app booking, hours and phone match EPFL; also notes classic tl/MBC stops plus new TAD stops, and minibuses of about 10 to 25 seats).
    • Primary EPFL page: EPFL - Public transportation. EPFL's « More information » / « Plus d'informations » pointer for TAD is the longer tl path tl, Transport à la demande (Ecublens also uses that path for its app link; its « Plus d'infos » short link is www.t-l.ch/transport-a-la-demande).
    • This does not replace m1 / bus 1 / 31 / MBC 701-705 for regular campus trips; it fills gaps in the west-Lausanne communes named above.
    • To verify: live wording on the tl transport à la demande web pages (short link and the longer path EPFL points to). Until those pages load cleanly for you, cite EPFL + Ecublens + the Plan-TAD PDF + the app store listings above.
    • To verify: CHF single-ticket amount for TAD itself (unpublished on EPFL and Ecublens; the published rule is a zones 11-12 ticket or pass, with no CHF figure). Mobilis single-ticket prices from other pages are not a published TAD fare.

Regional buses (e.g. MBC) and Mobilis zone tickets are sold through the same official channels (tl desk / CFF / carrier apps), use those, not third-party fare blogs.
