LAMal (compulsory health insurance) and student exemption

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Within 3 months of taking up residence

Anyone who becomes liable for compulsory Swiss health insurance must take it out within three months of that date (for example, the date you take up residence in Switzerland). If you enrol on time, cover and premiums are back-dated to the start of the obligation. If you miss the three-month deadline, cover generally starts only from registration and a premium supplement can apply unless you have a valid reason for the delay.

For students: within 3 months of taking up residence, either take out Swiss LAMal with an authorised insurer, or apply for the Vaud exemption (dispense) if you qualify — do not leave this open-ended.

Sources:

  • FOPH FAQ Requirement to take out insurance (Sept 2025): FAQ Take out insurance (PDF) — “You have to take out health insurance within three months of becoming liable for compulsory insurance (e.g. of the date you take up residence …)”
  • Overview for foreign students: FOPH – Health insurance: Foreign students in Switzerland

The default rule

People who take up residence in Switzerland are generally required to take out compulsory health insurance (LAMal / KVG) with an authorised Swiss insurer. Premiums and cost-sharing then follow the normal Swiss system.

Students from EU / EFTA / United Kingdom

Under the free-movement / social-security agreements, students from EU/EFTA/UK who are in Switzerland for education/training are not required to take out Swiss LAMal as long as they are not working and remain covered by their home-country system. They can receive treatment in Switzerland with a European Health Insurance Card (EHIC).

If they start working in Switzerland, they must take out Swiss health insurance (place-of-work principle).

Source: FOPH foreign-students page; same point in the FOPH FAQ above (“You do not have to take out insurance” for non-working EU/EFTA/UK students).

Do you still need to file a Vaud dispense if you have an EHIC?
To verify: still unclear on FOPH and Vaud alone (re-checked 2026-10-05). FOPH: non-working EU/EFTA/UK students are not required to take out Swiss LAMal and may use an EHIC for treatment in Switzerland. Vaud: when someone does request a dispense, an EHIC copy is accepted as proof of equivalent cover (alongside proof of student/trainee status). Neither page says EHIC alone is enough without any cantonal filing, nor that every EHIC holder must file the Vaud form. In practice, ask OVAM (Office vaudois de l’assurance-maladie) via the Vaud dispense service if you need formal confirmation so the canton does not treat you as uninsured. EPFL study pages that mention sending an EHIC copy to OVAM are campus guidance, not a substitute for FOPH/Vaud.

Links: FOPH foreign students · Vaud dispense request

Students and trainees from other countries

Students/trainees from outside EU/EFTA/UK can apply for exemption from Swiss compulsory insurance if they have private insurance with cover equivalent to a Swiss basic insurer. Applications go to the competent cantonal authority.

FOPH states exemption can be granted for three years, renewable for another three years; after that, Swiss insurance is required.

Before you leave home: ask your home insurer to stamp and sign Vaud’s equivalence control form (available via the Vaud dispense service), and keep proof of student/trainee status. File the Vaud request within the three-month window above.

Sources: FOPH page above; Demander une dispense … | État de Vaud

Applying in Canton of Vaud

Vaud offers an online service to request a dispense (exemption) from the insurance obligation for foreigners residing in the canton, when equivalent cover is proven (e.g. EHIC copy, or Vaud’s equivalence control form stamped/signed by the foreign insurer, plus proof of student/trainee status).

Start here: Demander une dispense de l'assurance maladie pour les étrangers résidents dans le canton | État de Vaud

FOPH publishes a list of cantonal bodies authorised to grant exemptions (PDF linked from the FOPH foreign-students page — check the latest file on that page).

Important: rules differ by nationality, work status, and whether cover is truly equivalent. Do not assume you are exempt until the cantonal authority confirms (where a request is required). Starting paid work can change the answer overnight (especially for EU/EFTA/UK students).

Household contents and liability insurance (sometimes required for housing) are separate from LAMal health insurance.
