B-permit renewal (student stay in Canton Vaud)

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Companion to Permit & commune registration. Orientation only, not immigration advice. Confirm every step with your commune’s Contrôle des habitants / Office de la population and, when needed, Service de la population (SPOP).

What this page is for

How to renew / prolong a student B residence permit while studying at EPFL and living in Canton Vaud: who you apply with, what official pages name as timing and documents, and where EU/EFTA vs third-country paths differ on those pages. First registration is on Permit & commune. Travel while waiting for a (new) card is on Travel without permit. Leaving Switzerland or changing address is on Leaving / deregister.

Who renews where (official chain)

  1. You stay responsible for a valid authorisation (communes and EPFL both stress this). EPFL is not part of the official renewal procedure.
  2. Before expiry, an Avis de fin de validité (expiry notice) is sent to your commune of domicile and/or to you. Canton welcome pages say federal authorities send it; Lausanne and several Vaud communes describe a convocation with that avis, typically about six weeks before expiry.
  3. You file the renewal at your commune of residence. The commune forwards the file to SPOP (Service de la population), which examines and decides.
  4. After SPOP decides to issue or renew, it may invite you to the Centre de biométrie (Lausanne / Flon) to record biometric data before the new card is produced and mailed.

Sources: vaud-bienvenue.ch, Titres de séjour · État de Vaud, Nouveau titre de séjour / biométrie · EPFL, Residence permit · Ville de Lausanne, Renouvellement permis B UE/AELE · Ville de Lausanne, Renouvellement permis B État Tiers

EU/EFTA vs third-country (only where official pages distinguish)

EU/EFTA students

  • SEM: B for students is typically one year, with yearly renewals until studies are completed if conditions (admission to a recognised institution, sufficient means, adequate health/accident insurance) still hold. SEM FAQ: apply for extension 14 days before expiry.
  • Vaud (studies page): stay for studies under ALCP Annex I art. 24; authorisation for the duration of studies, issued for one year and renewable. Initial document themes include arrival form, ID, study attestation with planned duration, durable financial proof (or guarantor form), etc.
  • Lausanne B UE/AELE renewal page names: completed/dated/signed avis B UE/AELE, colour copy of passport or national ID, copy of your 3 latest payslips or any document proving financial means, plus social-assistance fee-waiver attestation if that applies. Online via QR on the convocation, contact form, or post.

Third-country students

  • vaud-bienvenue.ch: third-country B generally one year, and “may be renewed for 2 years” (general B wording, not student-specific). At the end of a completed or interrupted formation, a temporary study authorisation is not renewed.
  • État de Vaud (études États tiers): initial conditions under LEI art. 27 / OASA arts. 23–24 (school confirmation, financial means, suitable accommodation, commitment to leave after the planned formation). In principle one formation or further training lasting at most eight years; derogations only if duly justified. SPOP rules entry and stay. The page’s document list is written mainly for first authorisation (study request form, enrolment, financial proof, etc.).
  • Lausanne B État Tiers renewal page names: completed/dated/signed avis B OASA, colour passport/ID copy, 3 latest payslips or any document proving financial means, form 1350 if employer / company name changed since the permit was issued, social-assistance fee-waiver attestation if applicable. Same timing and online/post options as the UE/AELE page.
  • Biometrics: third-country holders already use plastic cards; Vaud states they go to the biometric centre when the title is established or renewed when SPOP invites them. EU/EFTA credit-card format also goes through commune filing then possible biometric invitation; biometric data are valid 5 years (distinct from the permit’s own end date).

Student reading tip: Lausanne’s “3 payslips / employer change / form 1350” lines are written for people in employment. For a study B, use the avis checklist and “any document proving financial means”; EPFL says proof of means may be asked again at renewal. Bring a recent EPFL enrolment / attestation (IS-Academia) when your letter or commune asks for study proof.

Documents typically named on official renewal pages

Always follow your avis / convocation. Patterns that appear repeatedly:

  • Completed, dated, signed Avis de fin de validité (UE/AELE or OASA version as sent)
  • Valid passport or (EU/EFTA) national ID, often a colour copy for online/post filing
  • Current permit card (communes that still see you in person ask for it)
  • Proof of financial means (Lausanne: 3 latest payslips or other proof; EPFL: means proof may be required again; Vaud study pages and EPFL first-file lists also stress enrolment and means)
  • Recent enrolment / study attestation when studies are the purpose of stay (EPFL / Vaud study pages; confirm on your letter)
  • Swiss civil-status document if a civil-status event took place in Switzerland (named on some commune lists)
  • Payment of the commune / cantonal fee when asked (Lausanne: pay when filing if you had a convocation; amount not printed on the Lausanne B pages checked)

Example published commune fee table (Chavannes-des-Bois, not EPFL campus communes): B UE/AELE CHF 70 adult / 35 child; B third-country CHF 102 adult and child. That page also lists an employer stamp on the third-country B avis (worker-oriented). Treat as that commune’s published list, not a Vaud-wide student tariff.

Ecublens (students page, move within Vaud): if a stay-permit prolongation must be filed when arriving from another Vaud commune, published amounts include CHF 90 (UE/AELE) or CHF 122 (third country) instead of the lower “arrival only” fee. That is a move + prolongation line, not a full annual-renewal checklist.

To verify for your own commune (Ecublens, Renens, Chavannes-près-Renens, Saint-Sulpice, Epalinges, etc.): the exact annual B-renewal CHF amount and whether filing is counter-only, online, or post. Live commune pages checked for this kit publish arrival student fees (and for Ecublens, move + prolongation when arriving from another Vaud commune), not a standalone annual-renewal tariff for students who already live there. Open your commune’s Contrôle des habitants page or call the number on your avis.

After you file: waiting, travel, biometrics

  • Processing delay: vaud-bienvenue.ch states the new permit is not always issued immediately because of volume. Cantonal migration authorities do not call your stay into question while the request is being processed.
  • Travel before the new card arrives: if you must leave Switzerland without the renewed card, ask SPOP in advance for a Visa de retour (return visa). Same trap family as first arrival: a commune attestation is not a travel document. Details and dated return-visa fees: Travel without permit.
  • Biometrics: wait for SPOP’s invitation; do not book the Flon centre early. Bring the invitation, passport/ID, and old permit (it may be punched). Card is generally mailed registered to your domicile (FAQ: often within about 15 days after the biometric visit). You have three months after the biometric invitation to book (FAQ).
  • Keep your address current so the avis and the new card can reach you. Address / departure rules: Leaving / deregister.

End of studies (do not renew on autopilot)

  • vaud-bienvenue.ch: when a formation ends or is interrupted, the temporary study authorisation is not renewed.
  • EPFL: in the last month of your stay, inform the Residents’ registration office of your upcoming departure and contact the population office about whether you can leave. See Leaving / deregister.
  • Job-search stay after graduation (where it exists) is a different track from renewing a student B. Pointers: Working while studying.
