Start here: your first weeks

Each step links to its page. Deadlines are only those stated by official sources.

Stuck on a word? Open the Glossary (B, L, FRAC, LAMal, SPOP …).

Printable one-pager: First-weeks checklist (print / PDF), open and use your browser’s Print → Save as PDF.

Before you travel

  1. Housing: apply to student halls and the UNIL-EPFL portal early, and watch for scams. Student-hall deposits are usually 1 to 3 months' rent. → EPFL housing
  2. Health insurance plan: EU/EFTA/UK students, bring your EHIC. Non-EU students, ask your home insurer to stamp Vaud's equivalence control form before you leave, or plan to take out Swiss LAMal. → LAMal
  3. Pack your commune documents: passport, passport photos, birth certificate (marriage certificate if applicable), lease or landlord attestation, about CHF 200 cash. EU students also need proof of funding (EPFL states CHF 2,000 per month). → Permit & commune

Days 1–3

  1. Get a prepaid Swiss SIM (often only a passport is needed; confirm in-store). → Mobile phone
  2. Move in, and check which commune your address is in (often not Lausanne). → Permit & commune
  3. Plan your commute and buy tickets or a pass from official sources. → Getting around

First week (EPFL)

  1. Matriculation Days: get your CAMIPRO card and validate it at a terminal. Attend Welcome Day (compulsory for new Bachelor's and Master's students). → CAMIPRO · Semester dates
  2. Log in to IS-Academia and print an enrolment certificate (less than 3 months old). → EPFL first, then commune

Within days of arrival (use your commune’s deadline)

Commune registration deadlines (official pages; follow the commune on your lease):

  • Ecublens, Renens, Chavannes-près-Renens: 8 days
  • Lausanne: mostly 14 days (Swiss students: 8 days on the student page)
  • St-Sulpice: 8 days (commune arrival form + canton; the web page itself gives no number)
  • EPFL guidance: 14 days; Vaud canton: 8 days
  1. Register at your commune's Contrôle des habitants, use your commune’s deadline in the callout above (not a site-wide number). Lausanne: online only. Ask for the attestation, but don't travel abroad on it alone: it is not a travel document. → Permit & commune (checklist / commune sections)

By the Friday of week 2 (18 Sep 2026 / 5 Mar 2027)

  1. Submit the FRAC and register for every course on IS-Academia. → Semester dates

First month

  1. Open a bank account with your passport, enrolment proof and the commune attestation. Many Swiss bills then arrive as QR-bills: how to pay them → Bank · Paying bills
  2. Non-EU students: go to the SPOP biometric-data appointment when the letter arrives. → Permit & commune
  3. Want French classes? EPFL students take Language Centre modules for free. Next registration windows: 15 Nov to 11 Dec 2026 (winter intensive) / 15 Jan to 23 Feb 2027 (spring); late registrations are not accepted. → Language Centre

Within 3 months of taking up residence

  1. Take out Swiss LAMal or apply for the Vaud exemption (dispense). → LAMal

Later / only if it applies

  1. Serafe bill (private flats; in a student hall the establishment pays), the AVS invoice in spring (send your B permit for studies to be exempt), tax if you work, and the driving-licence exchange within 12 months if you will drive. → Serafe · AVS · Student tax · Driving licence

Lost your passport or permit card? Police declaration first, then your embassy (passport) or commune (permit duplicate). → Lost passport, ID or permit card

Install Alertswiss, add Vaud: the official app for sirens and public alerts. → Alertswiss, sirens and public alerts

Starting in February 2027? The same steps apply. Spring classes start on 22 Feb 2027, and the FRAC and course registration are due on 5 Mar 2027. → Semester dates

First 2 weeks

  • Permit and commune registration 1 to verify

    EPFL certificate first, then the commune (EPFL 14 days; Renens/canton 8, follow your commune); permit letters and residence tax.

  • Leaving Switzerland before your permit card arrives

    Attestation is not a travel document: multi-entry visa vs return visa, and why not to deregister for a short trip.

  • Lost or stolen passport, ID card or permit card in Switzerland 4 to verify

    Police declaration first (Lausanne / Police Ouest lausannois: CHF 40 Swiss, CHF 50 foreign document or permit), then your embassy for a foreign passport, the Vaud Centre de biométrie for Swiss documents, and a permit duplicate via your commune / SPOP.

  • Family, partner and childcare pointers (student permits)

    SEM / Vaud family reunification pointers for student B/L permits (third-country student B/L track is discretionary under LEI art. 44/45, not a right), plus EPFL and Lausanne childcare entry points. Fees and durations only when official pages publish them.

  • B-permit renewal (student stay in Canton Vaud) 1 to verify

    Renew / prolong a student B residence permit in Vaud: avis, commune filing, SPOP, biometrics, EU/EFTA vs third-country pointers. Orientation only; confirm with your commune and SPOP.

  • Address change within Canton Vaud (move between communes) 4 to verify

    Move between Vaud communes or change address in the same commune: Contrôle des habitants deadlines, eDéménagement, published fees, and what else to update. Orientation only; confirm with your communes.

  • CAMIPRO student card 1 to verify

    Getting and validating your EPFL card, what it does, and what to do if it is lost.

  • Campus Wi-Fi: epfl vs eduroam

    Which campus SSID to use on day 1: prefer epfl with your EPFL account; eduroam for partner campuses such as UNIL.

  • EPFL Library and printing (myPrint) 1 to verify

    Rolex Learning Center hours, free swisscovery registration, loan rules and late fees, plus the myPrint allowance, per-page prices and free scanning.

  • Learning French at EPFL: the Language Centre 3 to verify

    Free French modules for EPFL students (priority over other groups), staff fees, module types, 2026-27 registration windows (15 Nov to 11 Dec 2026 / 15 Jan to 23 Feb 2027) and options if you are not eligible.

  • Semester dates and key enrolment deadlines (2026–2027) 2 to verify

    2026–2027 semester dates, FRAC and course registration, exam withdrawal deadlines.

  • IS-Academia basics (course registration hub)

    How Bachelor / Master students reach IS-Academia, complete FRAC, register for courses, pay the semester invoice (CHF 780 / 2240), and the published CHF 50 late-registration desk fee (Master's projects Study management).

  • Getting a Swiss mobile phone number

    Prepaid vs subscription, ID requirements and carrier pages.

  • Swiss phone: prepaid, eSIM, and Swisscom (official pages) 1 to verify

    Day-1 prepaid vs eSIM with Swisscom as the worked example, plus Sunrise pointers, companion to Mobile phone.

  • Health & emergencies

    EPFL's 24/7 campus emergency line, 144 off campus, and student mental-health support.

  • Alertswiss, sirens and civil-protection alerts in Vaud

    Install Alertswiss and add Vaud, the two siren signals, the annual siren test (first Wednesday of February, 13:30 to 16:30), and Vaud emergency meeting points, notices and shelters.

  • EPFL mental health (Psy consultation + Social consultation)

    Campus psychotherapeutic consultation (CHUV @ EPFL): booking, ~200 CHF dual-quote vs Trust Point, and how it differs from Social consultation.

  • Finding a GP and a pharmacy (Vaud) 3 to verify

    Everyday care: insurance first, finding a family doctor, EPFL's Point Santé, the on-call line and pharmacies.

  • Dental care in Vaud: what LAMal covers, what it does not, and how to find a dentist 4 to verify

    Basic insurance (LAMal) and the EHIC generally do not pay the dentist: the narrow exceptions, accidents, optional dental insurance, how dental bills work, finding a dentist and the 24/7 on-call line 0848 133 133.
