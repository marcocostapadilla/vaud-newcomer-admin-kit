Serafe (radio / TV fee)

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Serafe collects Switzerland’s household radio and television fee. It funds public and regional broadcasting. You do not register yourself: billing uses data from the residents’ registers after you register with your commune.

Practical points

  • If you live in a recognised student hostel / collective household, you normally should not receive a personal Serafe bill, the establishment pays. If you get a bill anyway, contact Serafe and/or your housing office.
  • One household, one fee. All adults in the household are jointly liable.
  • The fee is not tied to owning a TV or radio. Since 1 January 2024, opting out because you have no reception device is no longer possible.
  • Amounts (re-checked 5 Oct 2026 on serafe.ch/en and OFCOM FAQ, re-check before paying):
    • Private household: CHF 335 per year
    • Collective household (e.g. student hostel / boarding school): CHF 670 per year, paid by the institution, not by each resident individually (OFCOM: student hostels are collective households; the body responsible for the household pays)
    • From 1 January 2027, Serafe publishes a lower private-household fee (CHF 312 on serafe.ch), confirm on the official site before that date.
  • If you share a private flat, the household gets one bill (all adult names may appear).
  • You can request quarterly invoicing (details on Serafe / OFCOM FAQ). Paper quarterly invoices may carry a small extra charge; electronic options are described on the official sites.
  • Address errors: correct your data at the commune first; Serafe is fed from the residents’ register.
