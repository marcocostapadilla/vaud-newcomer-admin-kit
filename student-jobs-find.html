Finding a student job on / near EPFL (after the permit rules)

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

This page is where to look, not a substitute for Working while studying (15 h / 6-month wait / SDE-SPOP).

Read the rules page first

Before you apply anywhere:

  • Cap: 15 hours/week during courses, full-time in academic holidays (SEM / OASA / EPFL).
  • EU/EFTA: may start from the beginning of the stay; third-country: usually six months after studies start (EPFL notes an exception for student assistants in research linked to the field of study).
  • Procedure for B-permit regular paid work: employer form → Student Services Desk certificate → competent office (address on the form).

Full detail: Working while studying · EPFL - Working

EPFL also warns the study load is about 50-60 hours/week including personal study, so reconciling a job is hard.

Campus / EPFL-linked channels

Students Development Factory (SDF)

IT Services unit that hires students as student assistants under LEX 4.7.1 for admin tasks and software POCs.

EPFL states (SDF page):

  • Gross hourly wage: CHF 25.-
  • Workload should not exceed 15 hours per week
  • Hiring is mostly word of mouth; sometimes via the EPFL job board / petites annonces
  • Contact group: sdf-admins at groupes.epfl.ch (see live page for the exact address format)

Source: EPFL - Students Development Factory (SDF)

EPFL Library student assistants

The Library recruits student assistants each semester (EPFL, UNIL, HES, etc.). Engagements last 6 months and can be renewed. When posts are open, apply with CV + motivation letter in French to library@epfl.ch:

  • Autumn semester: May-July
  • Spring semester: December-January

Check the live page for whether posts are open (empty when none).

Source: EPFL Library - Offres d'emploi et recrutements

Junior Entreprise EPFL / Junior Experience

Student association platform for student jobs and JE projects: jx.epfl.ch · je.epfl.ch jobs. JE says project managers handle contractual framing for JE jobs; you still must stay inside your permit / hour rules (Working).

Career Centre graduate jobs board

epflcareer.ch Jobs is aimed more at graduate / career listings (CED). It does not replace the Working / internship authorisation pages.

Labs and units

Many labs post one-off student-assistant ads on their own pages or actu.epfl.ch. Treat each posting as a lead, then run the EPFL Working checklist.

Budget sanity check (EPFL figures)

On the Working page EPFL illustrates: average hourly wage CHF 25, 8 h/week → about CHF 800/month, against a monthly budget around CHF 2300, so you still need roughly CHF 1500/month from other sources. Treat as EPFL’s illustration, not a promise that every job pays CHF 25 (SDF / LEX assistant rate is CHF 25 gross when that directive applies).

Source: EPFL - Working

Tax / LAMal reminders when you start earning

  • Withholding tax and possible refund if annual income ≤ CHF 28,800 (EPFL Working page; confirm with Vaud tax office).
  • Starting work can change LAMal / EHIC exemption status → LAMal.
  • Student tax track → Student tax.

What this page does not do

  • It does not list every open vacancy.
  • It does not certify that myjob.epfl.ch (older AGEPoly portal mentioned in old EPFL news) is still the main board; prefer SDF, Library, JE, Career Centre, and unit ads, since its current status is not confirmed on an official page.
  • It does not waive SDE/SPOP steps for third-country students.
