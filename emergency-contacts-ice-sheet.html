Emergency contacts and ICE sheet for Vaud

Last checked: 11 October 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

One page with the Swiss and Vaud emergency and urgent numbers, how to prepare your phone so helpers can reach your contacts, and a checklist to print and keep in your wallet. Each number links to the page on this site that explains it in depth.

Key facts

  • Life-threatening medical emergency: call 144 (État de Vaud, Urgences).
  • Not life-threatening but cannot wait: call the Vaud on-call doctors line (Centrale téléphonique des médecins de garde, CTMG) on 0848 133 133, local rate. A nurse assesses you and directs you to a doctor, a pharmacy or the emergency department (État de Vaud; Unisanté).
  • Emergency short numbers routed by the federal telecom office (OFCOM): 112, 117, 118, 142, 143, 144, 147.
  • Rega air rescue: 1414 in Switzerland, +41 333 333 333 from abroad (Rega).
  • Poisoning: 145, Tox Info Suisse (Tox Info Suisse).

  • 142 is a support line, not a rescue line. In immediate danger call 117, 144 or 118 (État de Vaud, 142 news).
  • On the EPFL campus, use the campus emergency numbers described in Health and emergencies.
  • For sirens and official alerts, see Alertswiss and civil protection.
  • Rega says there is no "wrong" number: Swiss rescue services pass calls on to each other (Rega).

2. ICE contacts and a medical ID on your phone

  • ICE means "in case of emergency": a contact saved in your phone that helpers or the hospital can call.
  • Rega considers saving an ICE number useful, but says it mostly helps after the rescue, at the hospital, not at the accident site (Rega, emergency numbers FAQ).
  • Most phones let you fill in an emergency medical record (medical ID) with allergies, medicines, blood group and emergency contacts, readable from the lock screen. To set it up, follow your phone maker's help: Apple, Medical ID in the Health app or Google, emergency information on Android.
  • Turn on the option that shows the medical ID on the locked screen, otherwise helpers cannot see it.
  • Pick one contact who lives in Switzerland and one at home, and write their numbers with the country code (+41 for Switzerland).
  • Rega app: it sends your position to the Rega operations centre and opens a call. It needs location sharing switched on, a SIM card and at least minimal network coverage (Rega, Rega app).
