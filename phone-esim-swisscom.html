What this page adds

The main phone page lists Swisscom / Sunrise / Salt prepaid hubs. This draft focuses on eSIM vs physical SIM, with Swisscom as the worked example (largest public docs), plus short official pointers for Sunrise. EPFL’s “Mobile subscription” pages are staff IT (Swisscom NATEL go for EPFL-assigned numbers), useful only if you later get an EPFL work phone, not for a student day-1 SIM.

Day-1 choice (student newcomer)

Option When it helps Official starting point
Prepaid + physical SIMBuy in a shop with passport; works on almost every phoneCarrier prepaid pages on phone.html
Prepaid + eSIMPhone supports eSIM; you want activation without waiting for a plastic cardSwisscom / Sunrise eSIM help below
Monthly subscriptionLonger stay, predictable data; more ID / credit checksSame carrier sites, re-check terms

Swisscom’s newcomers / expat page states you can start with an eSIM and activate online, and that with valid ID you can get started even before a permanent residence permit; many newcomers start on prepaid and switch later. Confirm live: Swisscom, Mobile, Internet & TV for expats.

Swisscom prepaid vs eSIM

Prepaid overview: no fixed monthly fee; top up as you go; also available as eSIM. If unused for 12 months, Swisscom states the SIM/eSIM becomes invalid and remaining credit lapses (GTC for Mobile Prepaid).
→ Swisscom Prepaid

eSIM help (Swisscom): an eSIM is digital (no plastic). Swisscom states eSIMs are currently free of charge (vs a CHF 40 SIM activation fee mentioned on that help page for physical SIMs, re-check the live figure). Prepaid eSIM: order/set up in My Swisscom; subscription eSIM: select eSIM when ordering, then QR / My Swisscom app.
→ Swisscom, Order, set up and use a Swisscom eSIM

Practical tip: keep a foreign SIM in a dual-SIM / dual-eSIM phone until your Swiss number and TWINT / banking apps work. Porting rules: carrier-specific, To verify on the carrier’s number-porting page before you cancel anything.

EPFL mobile (staff only, do not confuse)

EPFL IT documents EPFL-assigned Swisscom subscriptions (NATEL go Swiss premium) and Cockpit data packs for staff/service phones. Students needing a personal number should use consumer prepaid/subscription pages above, not Service Now staff forms.
