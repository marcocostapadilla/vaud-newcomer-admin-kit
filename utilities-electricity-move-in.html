Electricity when you move into a private flat (SiL / Romande Energie)

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Who this is for

Students in a private flat or room where the lease makes the occupant responsible for electricity (and sometimes heat/water/multimedia). Student halls often bill utilities differently; check your hall contract first.

Your commune address decides the grid company. Rough starter map (confirm on the lease / meter / landlord):

Area (typical) Utility to announce move-in with Official start
City of Lausanne networkSiL (Services industriels de Lausanne)SiL déménagement
Much of western Vaud (Ecublens, Renens, Chavannes, St-Sulpice, Morges area, etc.)Romande EnergieEspace client / changement d'adresse

To verify: ask the landlord or check the meter / previous bills if the building is on a different supplier (some buildings use another distributor). Do not assume every EPFL-area flat is Romande Energie.

Lausanne network: SiL

SiL pages for individuals:

  • Hub: Déménagement
  • Service: Changement d'adresse - annonce aux Services industriels
  • New arrival: Nouvel arrivant

Official rule (SiL): announce any address change at least 15 working days in advance so everything is ready when you arrive. Individuals: use the online service / mySiL portal (paths linked from the déménagement hub: nouvel arrivant, stay on SiL network, or leave the network).

SiL can cover electricity and, depending on the building, other products (heat, gas, multimedia). Open the live SiL product pages for what your address has; cite only official package prices.

Romande Energie network

FAQ (Espace client):

  • Who announces: the occupant. The agency is not obliged to do it (they may help).
  • Which date: the key handover / entry or exit état des lieux date.
  • How: déménagement form in the Espace client or on the website.
  • Lead time: you cannot announce a move-in date retroactively; Romande Energie requires two days (excluding weekends and public holidays) before arrival/departure for the meter reading and contract activation.

Sources: FAQ Espace client · Bienvenue chez vous · Votre électricité

On move-in you get a default tariff; you can later ask for a tariff that fits your use (simple vs dual meter). Product choice (e.g. Énergie Suisse / Énergie Romande) is separate from the meter tariff. kWh prices: use Romande Energie’s live tariff pages for your locality; ElCom oversees pricing (company wording). Do not copy a CHF/kWh figure into this kit without a dated quote from that page.
