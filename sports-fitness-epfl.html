Sports and fitness (UNIL+EPFL Sport Santé)

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Companion to CAMIPRO & matriculation. Prices and hours change; confirm on sportsanteunilepfl.ch / sport.unil.ch before you rely on a figure.

What this page is for

How EPFL students use the UNIL+EPFL Sports Centre (Centre sportif universitaire de Dorigny), who pays what, and how to create a Sport Santé login to book classes. This is not the same as the compulsory CHF 16 “sports facilities contribution” on your EPFL semester invoice.

Short version

  • Enrolled UNIL / EPFL / AISTS students (and UNIL doctoral students / Swiss-university students) with a valid student card: free access to the Dorigny sports centre and to free classes (category A). Bring your CAMIPRO (EPFL student card).
  • Create a free Sport Santé personal account to register for free classes that need a booking, buy paid courses, or buy optional subscriptions (weight room, yoga, etc.).
  • EPFL doctoral students are category C: Sport Santé access card CHF 120 per half-year or CHF 200 per year (not free like Bachelor / Master category A).
  • Exchange students and other category B profiles: CHF 50 / half-year or CHF 80 / year for the access card.

Sources: Conditions d’accès (SSUE) · Programme 2026–27 EN PDF

Where and how to start

Step What to do
1Read the live programme and class list on sport.unil.ch / sportsanteunilepfl.ch (redirects to sport.unil.ch).
2Create your Sport Santé login (free for students) when you need bookings or paid offers.
3For free drop-in classes: go with your valid student card (CAMIPRO).
4For booked free classes: Login → reserve → attend.
5For paid courses / camps / weight-room options: Login → purchase → pay.

Registration opens (programme 2026–27): 15 August for autumn-semester courses, 15 January for spring-semester courses.

Secretariat / Villa des Sports: sports@unil.ch, +41 21 692 21 50, Centre sportif de Dorigny, CH-1015 Lausanne. Contact page (checked 5 Oct 2026): from 5 Oct 2026, secretariat Mon–Thu 09:30–12:30 (earlier autumn hours were Mon–Thu 10:00–18:00 until 1 Oct 2026). Sports centre building on that same contact page: Mon–Fri 07:00–22:30, weekends 10:00–18:00; closed on Vaud public holidays. Payment for cards / courses: online (credit card, Postcard, Twint) or at the secretariat (cash, Twint, bank card).

Weekend hours (two official wordings): the live Contact et accès page lists weekends 10:00–18:00 (SSUE conditions art. 1.2 point hours to that page). The 2026–27 programme PDF “Annual opening” table (14 Sep 2026 → 4 Jul 2027) lists weekends 08:00–19:00; the same PDF’s August–September opening window lists weekends 10:00–18:00. Prefer the live contact page before a weekend visit.

Sources: Contact et accès · Programme 2026–27 EN PDF · Conditions d’accès (SSUE)

Annual card validity (conditions page): 1 September → 30 September of the following year. Half-year: 1 September → 28 February or 1 February → 30 September.

One-off access for outdoor offers without a Sport Santé card: CHF 25 (1 day), 50 (2–3 days), 100 (4+ days).

Lockers (home page + conditions): CHF 50 / academic year (1 July → 15 July next year) or CHF 35 / half-year (1 Sep → 28 Feb / 1 Feb → 15 Jul). Conditions art. 7 also states a personal locker at CHF 50 for the academic year online or at Villa des Sports.

You need accident insurance and civil liability (RC); SSUE declines responsibility in case of accident. Which insurance pays a sports accident (LAMal accident cover or an employer's AANP): Accident insurance for students.

Campus lunchtime sessions (EPFL)

PolyDoc (EPFL association page pointing to UNIL-EPFL Sports): free supervised lunchtime sessions on the EPFL campus (registration mandatory, free). “Shot” sessions 12:00–12:30 and 12:30–13:00 (30 min); “campus” sessions 12:15–13:00 (45 min). Organised by Sports Universitaires UNIL-EPFL; PolyDoc credits Campus Durable and DSPS. Details and booking: Sports Centre website.

Source: PolyDoc, Sports
