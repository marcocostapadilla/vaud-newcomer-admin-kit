First rule: look up your commune

Waste rules in Vaud are communal (with regional “périmètre” systems). Your flat’s address determines:

  • whether you use taxed bags or taxe au poids (weight-based),
  • collection days,
  • déchèterie / écopoint locations,
  • what goes in which stream.

If you live in a student hall, ask the hall what they provide (some buildings already have containers and calendars). EPFL’s campus sits across several communes (often Ecublens, Lausanne, Renens, St-Sulpice, and others) — studying at EPFL does not mean your bags follow Lausanne rules.

Canton-level context (Cour des comptes, rapport n° 85): as of end 2023, more than 85% of the Vaud population, in 237 communes, used the regional taxed-bag system « Trier c’est valoriser »; other communes use different arrangements (including weight-based taxation).

Taxed bags (« Trier c’est valoriser »)

In communes that joined the harmonised regional system:

  • Household rubbish that cannot be recycled goes in the official white bag labelled « Trier c’est valoriser » (Lausanne: other colours such as grey, black, transparent are forbidden for household refuse).
  • Bags are sold in local shops in participating communes.
  • The regional site states that bag prices are identical across the region (same roll prices as published by Ville de Lausanne).

Price table mismatch to watch

Commune d’Ecublens still publishes a taxe-au-sac table on its déchets page with 35 L = CHF 2.00 (roll CHF 20.00), while Lausanne’s live table shows 35 L = CHF 1.95. Other sizes match (1.00 / 3.80 / 6.00).

Lausanne only — taxe de base

Ville de Lausanne also describes a taxe de base on building volume paid by owners (may be passed to tenants via the lease): CHF 0.24 HT per m³ on that page. This amount is Lausanne-specific — other communes set their own taxe de base (or refund it — Ecublens states its taxe de base is reimbursed as a social measure).

Source: same Lausanne sacs taxés page

What usually goes for separate collection

Exact streams and schedules are commune-specific. Typical separate streams in Vaud (confirm locally):

  • Paper / cardboard
  • Glass
  • PET / aluminium / tin (often shop return or écopoints)
  • Organic / biodéchets (where a separate collection exists — e.g. Ecublens compostables)
  • Batteries, electronics, textiles, bulky waste (déchèterie / special collections; shops must take back similar products they sell — Ecublens flyer)

Canton communications (Mar 2026 communiqué on the 2024 composition study) stress that a large share of what still ends up in taxed bags could have been sorted (biodéchets and plastics especially).

Source: État de Vaud — “autopsie” des ordures ménagères

Practical first-week steps

  1. Identify your commune from your address (it may not be Lausanne even if you study at EPFL).
  2. Open the commune’s déchets / recyclage page (or ask the housing office / hall).
  3. Buy the correct official bags if your commune uses taxe au sac — or learn how taxe au poids works if it does not.
  4. Note collection days and nearest écopoint / déchèterie (To verify on your commune site — hours above are commune-specific snapshots).
  5. For Lausanne: bag rules and taxe de base are on lausanne.ch — sacs taxés. For Ecublens / Renens / St-Sulpice: use the commune links in the section above.
