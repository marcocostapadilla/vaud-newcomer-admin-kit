First rule: look up your commune

Waste rules in Vaud are communal (with regional “périmètre” systems). Your flat’s address determines:

  • whether you use taxed bags or taxe au poids (weight-based),
  • collection days,
  • déchèterie / écopoint locations,
  • what goes in which stream.

If you live in a student hall, ask the hall what they provide (some buildings already have containers and calendars). EPFL’s campus sits across several communes (often Ecublens, Lausanne, Renens, St-Sulpice, and others), studying at EPFL does not mean your bags follow Lausanne rules.

Canton-level context (Cour des comptes, rapport n° 85): as of end 2023, more than 85% of the Vaud population, in 237 communes, used the regional taxed-bag system « Trier c’est valoriser »; other communes use different arrangements (including weight-based taxation).

Canton legal framework (system, not shelf prices)

État de Vaud explains pollueur-payeur / causalité and points to LGD art. 30a (quantity-based tax + base tax) on the DGE financement page. That page does not list shop bag prices. It explains why taxed bags exist; for prices, use your commune's page.

  • État de Vaud: Déchets (hub)
  • État de Vaud: Gérer les déchets (financement, prévention, information)
  • DGE notice PDF linked from that page: GEODE-Gestion-Dechets-WEB.pdf (communal financing guide; not a consumer price list)

Bag prices still vary by system and by published commune table (Lausanne and Ecublens are cited separately below; they disagree on 35 L). Check your commune page and the shop shelf; there is no single kit-wide CHF figure.

Taxed bags (« Trier c’est valoriser »)

In communes that joined the harmonised regional system:

  • Household rubbish that cannot be recycled goes in the official white bag labelled « Trier c’est valoriser » (Lausanne: other colours such as grey, black, transparent are forbidden for household refuse).
  • Bags are sold in local shops in participating communes.
  • Prices differ by commune. Commune pages disagree on the 35 L bag (Lausanne CHF 1.95 vs Ecublens CHF 2.00, 5 Oct 2026), so check the table for the commune on your lease.

EPFL-area commune pointers (official pages only)

Use these as starting points; hours and calendars change, download the current dépliant from the commune site.

Ecublens (many EPFL addresses)

  • Hub: Ecublens, Gestion des déchets
  • Official taxed bags required; points to vaud-taxeausac.ch; VALORSA périmètre. Bag prices: see Ecublens table above (35 L = CHF 2.00), not the Lausanne table.
  • Déchèterie (Chemin de Verney 15): Mon & Wed 13:30–17:30 (until 18:00 1 Apr–31 Oct); Sat 09:30–16:30. Access badge required, Ecublens states CHF 10 per badge at the service des habitants (Place du Motty 4), max one per inhabitant; refundable if returned working.
  • ECOpoints (chemin du Bochet; site scolaire du Pontet): Mon–Sat 07:00–20:00.
  • Door-to-door compostables collection (Transvoirie SA, weekly), brown PEHD containers; no bags left at the roadside.
  • Mobile déchèterie: last Saturday of the month 08:00–12:00 at Pontet (ferraille, wood, pierreux, bulky &gt; 60 cm).

Source: Ecublens page above (hours and badge price as published 5 Oct 2026 fetch).

Renens

  • Renens, Collecte des déchets (confirmed 5 Oct 2026: household refuse in taxed bags on Tuesday (zone 1) or Thursday (zone 2), biowaste Monday plus Thursday from June to September, paper/cardboard Wednesday, glass one Friday a month; all 2026 dates in the Info-déchets 2026 leaflet (PDF))
  • Renens, Comment trier ?
  • Renens uses taxed bags + a communal taxe de base framework (2013 règlement / préavis). Per-person CHF is not published there (budget totals only; not a per-person rate).

VAT of 17 L CHF 1.25, 35 L CHF 2.50, 60 L CHF 4.75, 110 L CHF 7.50 (legal caps, not shop prices; the Renens page lists the white bag in 35, 60 and 110 L), and a maximum taxe de base of CHF 120 per year incl. VAT per inhabitant over 18, set by your situation on 1 January. Renens publishes no shelf price or current base tax amount (checked 5 Oct 2026), so both stay To verify. What usually goes for separate collection

Exact streams and schedules are commune-specific. Typical separate streams in Vaud (confirm locally):

  • Paper / cardboard
  • Glass
  • PET / aluminium / tin (often shop return or écopoints)
  • Organic / biodéchets (where a separate collection exists, e.g. Ecublens compostables)
  • Batteries, electronics, textiles, bulky waste (déchèterie / special collections; shops must take back similar products they sell, Ecublens flyer)

Canton communications (Mar 2026 communiqué on the 2024 composition study) stress that a large share of what still ends up in taxed bags could have been sorted (biodéchets and plastics especially).

Source: État de Vaud, “autopsie” des ordures ménagères

Practical first-week steps

  1. Identify your commune from your address (it may not be Lausanne even if you study at EPFL).
  2. Open the commune’s déchets / recyclage page (or ask the housing office / hall).
  3. Buy the correct official bags if your commune uses taxe au sac, or learn how taxe au poids works if it does not.
  4. Note collection days and nearest écopoint / déchèterie (To verify on your commune site, hours above are commune-specific snapshots).
  5. For Lausanne: bag rules and taxe de base are on lausanne.ch, sacs taxés. For Ecublens / Renens / St-Sulpice: use the commune links in the section above.
