Health & emergencies

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Pointers only, not medical advice. Confirm numbers and procedures on the official pages.

  • On EPFL campus (24/7): call EPFL’s emergency line +41 21 693 30 00 (from an EPFL landline: 115), or use SOS in the EPFL Campus app. EPFL asks you to use these campus numbers for a faster on-site response rather than dialling 144 or 112 first while on campus, see EPFL, Health emergencies and EPFL, Rescue / emergencies.
  • Off campus, life-threatening medical emergency: 144 (ambulance). General overview of campus rescue: same EPFL emergencies pages above.
  • Victim of violence or another offence: call 142, the national victim-support line (since 1 May 2026; free, confidential, 24/7). 142 is not an emergency number: in immediate danger call 117 (police), 144 (ambulance) or 118 (fire). Next steps and Vaud LAVI centres: Free legal advice: victim support · État de Vaud, 142.
  • Sirens and public alerts: the annual siren test, the Alertswiss app and what to do if a real alert comes: see Alertswiss, sirens and public alerts.
  • Vaccinations (adult catch-up, flu, COVID, measles; after an animal bite, go to an emergency department): see Vaccinations in Vaud.
  • Dentist or dental costs: dental care is mostly not covered by LAMal; finding a dentist and the 24/7 dental on-call line: see Dental care and LAMal.
  • Student mental-health support (appointments): EPFL, Psychotherapeutic consultation (CHUV psychiatrists/psychologists on campus; emergencies: campus numbers above, or 144 off campus).
  • EPFL Social consultation (Consultation sociale): free, neutral, confidential help for students and PhDs (stress, admin, money/scholarships, health-related study issues, referrals). French or English; campus or online. Email consultation.sociale@epfl.ch. EPFL: emails are checked every business day, with a response within 48 hours. EPFL Social consultation · FR: Consultation sociale. Money problems? See Money help for students.
