Health & emergencies

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Pointers only — not medical advice. Confirm numbers and procedures on the official pages.

  • On EPFL campus (24/7): call EPFL’s emergency line +41 21 693 30 00 (from an EPFL landline: 115), or use SOS in the EPFL Campus app. EPFL asks you to use these campus numbers for a faster on-site response rather than dialling 144 or 112 first while on campus — see EPFL — Health emergencies and EPFL — Rescue / emergencies.
  • Off campus, life-threatening medical emergency: 144 (ambulance). General overview of campus rescue: same EPFL emergencies pages above.
  • Student mental-health support (appointments): EPFL — Psychotherapeutic consultation (CHUV psychiatrists/psychologists on campus; emergencies: campus numbers above, or 144 off campus).
