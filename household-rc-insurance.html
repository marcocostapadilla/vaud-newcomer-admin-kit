Landlords and halls often ask for household + RC. That does not replace LAMal.

Household insurance (assurance ménage)

EPFL (English housing-insurance page): covers water damage, broken glass, and theft (including theft outside the home) in your apartment, and water damage in a neighbour’s flat (theft/glass wording applies to your apartment; neighbour coverage is water damage only).

  • Not mandatory by federal law as stated by EPFL, but almost always required by rental agencies and student halls.
  • Often included in student-hall contracts — ask your hall.
  • Cancellable in writing with 3 months’ notice before the end date (EPFL).

Source: EPFL — Insurances for housing

Private liability (RC / responsabilité civile)

EPFL (English third-party liability page; French RC page matches): covers you or a family member for physical injury or material damage caused to a third party, and helps against unjustified claims.

EPFL states it is compulsory in Switzerland if you want to:

  • rent accommodation, or
  • ride a bicycle.

Also highly recommended otherwise; claims can reach large amounts (EPFL: cover up to several million francs). Often sold together with household insurance. If you still live with your parents, their private RC may cover you (EPFL). EPFL EN: cancellable in writing with 3 months’ notice before the end date. (EPFL FR: “préavis de 3 mois selon la durée [du] contrat”.)

Sources:

  • EPFL — Third party liability insurance
  • EPFL — Assurance responsabilité civile privée

EPFL’s study-cost table lists RC as about CHF 10 / month (CHF 120 / year) as a budget line — treat as an estimate; compare offers yourself.

Source: EPFL — Financement des études

Vaud: ECA fire and natural-disaster insurance (compulsory)

For the main EPFL campus in Vaud, EPFL states:

  • After you register with your commune, the ECA (Cantonal Establishment of Insurance) is informed and sends a letter so you insure household contents / personal property against fire and natural disasters.
  • This insurance is compulsory for all residents of the canton (EPFL cites art. 27 LAIEN). ECA’s own mobilier-ménage page likewise states the LAIEN obligation for residents (including tenants / student housing).
  • EPFL: “Don’t underestimate this amount, as the insurance is really not expensive (from CHF 25/year in canton of Vaud).”

If you live in a furnished flat, check with the landlord whether furnishings are already covered by their policy. ECA still expects you to insure your own personal belongings.

Source: EPFL — Insurances for housing (ECA details and other cantons for extended campuses)
EPFL links: ECA — Assurance mobilier ménage · ECA regional agencies

To verify: current ECA premium for your inventory / letter — use the ECA letter once it arrives; do not invent a personal amount from this kit. EPFL’s “from CHF 25/year” is a campus ballpark, not your quote.

Practical order

  1. Sort LAMal / dispense within 3 months of residence → LAMal.
  2. When signing a lease or hall contract, check whether ménage + RC are included or required.
  3. Expect an ECA letter after commune registration if you live in Vaud.
  4. Cross-check deposits / blocked accounts on EPFL housing and the expand Rental deposit / garantie de loyer draft if present.
