Leaving or changing address (commune departure)

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Confirm every step with your commune’s Contrôle des habitants / Office de la population and, for permit questions, SPOP.

What this page is for

Short orientation when you:

  • move to another commune (or canton), or
  • leave Switzerland at the end of studies / for a long break.

Arrival rules are on Permit & commune. Travel before the permit card arrives is on Travel without permit.

Canton baseline (Vaud)

  • Arrivals: announce within 8 days in the new commune (cantonal rule). Your commune page may match EPFL’s 14 days (e.g. Lausanne for many foreign students) or be stricter (8 days in Ecublens, Renens, Chavannes-près-Renens). Always follow the commune on your lease.
  • Départs / address changes: announce to the Contrôle des habitants of your commune of residence. A landlord / gérance announcement does not replace your own declaration.
  • eDéménagement: if your current and future communes participate, you can often announce online. If only the departure commune is in the system, you may announce departure online but still finish arrival at the new commune’s counter. Do not double-file (online + in person) unless asked.

Sources:

  • État de Vaud, Changement d'adresse
  • État de Vaud, Annoncer son changement d'adresse (eDéménagement)

Leaving Switzerland (end of stay)

EPFL: during the last month of your stay in Switzerland, inform the Residents’ registration office of your upcoming departure, and contact the population office to confirm whether you can leave the country.

Source: EPFL, Residence permit (section Definitively)

Practical follow-ups newcomers often forget (pointers only, confirm each with the issuer):

  • Cancel or transfer LAMal / Vaud dispense status if it applies → LAMal
  • Serafe follows the residents’ register in private flats → Serafe
  • Bank, phone, lease end date, rent-deposit release → Bank · Rental deposit
  • Forwarding address with Poste (To verify on poste.ch before you rely on a product name)

Temporary absence (internship / project abroad)

If you tell the commune you are leaving without keeping a Swiss home address, EPFL says you will need a new residence permit (and possibly a new visa) on return. Prefer keeping a principal address in Vaud for short absences when the commune allows it, see EPFL’s Temporarily for holidays, internship, project abroad and Staying in another canton sections.

Source: EPFL, Residence permit
