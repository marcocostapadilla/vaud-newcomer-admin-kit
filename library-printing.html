EPFL Library and printing (myPrint)

What this page is for

How to start using the EPFL Library in the Rolex Learning Center (RLC), and how printing, copying and scanning work and what they cost. Your CAMIPRO card is both your library card and your printing badge.

Library basics (Rolex Learning Center)

  • Opening hours: the Library and the RLC are open every day, 07:00 to midnight, except 1 August and 25 December, and open to everyone. Service desk: Monday to Friday 08:00 to 23:45, weekends 09:00 to 23:45. Phone answers Monday to Friday 09:00 to 12:00 and 14:00 to 17:00; emails answered within 24 hours (working days). library@epfl.ch, +41 21 693 21 56.
  • Getting there: M1 metro (EPFL or UNIL-Sorge), tl bus 1 (Pâqueret), tl 31 (Argand / Pâqueret), MBC 701 (Pâqueret). Bike and scooter parking outside the building (Bikes at EPFL).
  • Study spaces: 1,175 workplaces in the RLC, 786 of them in the Library, in silent (no talking) and quiet (low voices) areas. Live occupancy in the Affluences app. Ten meeting "bubbles" can be booked by the EPFL / UNIL community, every day 08:00 to 22:00. The Cube (screen-equipped room) is booked in Affluences for 2 to 4 people, at most 1 week ahead and 2 hours per day.
  • House rules in short: no audible phone calls; closed drinks or lids only, no messy food; one workspace per person; do not move furniture. Lockers are in the basement, and the Library is not responsible for belongings left unattended.
  • Computers: all Library computers are free. Research terminals need no login; the others use your EPFL (GASPAR) login.
  • Loans of small equipment at the desk: headphones, power banks, USB sticks, mice, scientific calculators, adapters and power strips.

Source: EPFL Library, Practical information

Register once (swisscovery), then borrow with CAMIPRO

Registration is free and online, and you do not need to visit the desk afterwards:

  1. Create a SWITCH edu-ID account (or log in if you have one). Use a private email address, not your EPFL one, to secure the account.
  2. Link your EPFL account in SWITCH edu-ID (log in with your GASPAR credentials).
  3. Create your swisscovery account and accept the terms.

Then borrow at the self-service loan terminals with your CAMIPRO card, which also works in all swisscovery libraries (about 500 in Switzerland). You do not need a Swiss postal address to create a SWITCH edu-ID.

Loan rules (5 Oct 2026):

  • 28 days for most documents; 14 days for the Teaching Collection; 7 days for journals, DVDs, video games and consoles; 1 day for equipment (not renewable). Up to 100 items at a time.
  • 5 automatic extensions if nobody has reserved the item.
  • Return at the desk or in the white boxes in the Library, any time.
  • Late fees (reminders by email from noreply@slsp.ch): first reminder free, one working day after the due date; then CHF 5 per document, another CHF 5, then another CHF 10, one week apart (shorter gaps for 7-day and 1-day loans). Pay at the desk (cash, CAMIPRO, card or TWINT). Unpaid fees are invoiced after 2 months, and an invoice still unpaid 30 days after the second reminder blocks your account. Questions: contentieux.bib@epfl.ch, +41 21 693 41 00.
  • Reservations and deliveries for EPFL members: EPFL items and items from other swisscovery libraries are free if you pick them up at the EPFL Library desk; CHF 8 for pickup at another swisscovery library; CHF 12 for posting to your address. Scans of an article or chapter (up to 50 pages) are free for EPFL members.

City libraries: Lausanne's municipal libraries are free and separate from EPFL Library: see Culture on a student budget.

Sources: EPFL Library, Practical information (registration) · EPFL Library, Find and access documents (loans, fees)

Printing, copying and scanning (myPrint)

Your account: every enrolled student automatically has a myPrint account; you need it to use EPFL printers. Your CAMIPRO card has two purses: the normal CAMIPRO purse (cafeterias, shops) and a separate myPrint purse used only for printing.

Semester allowance (as published on 5 Oct 2026): students enrolled in a semester usually get CHF 24 per semester, about 600 black-and-white A4 pages, double-sided. New students receive it a few days before the semester starts; returning students about 2 to 3 weeks after it starts. PhD students, doctoral assistants and exchange students do not get it. Some schools add extra. EPFL says the amount can change without notice and is not a right. Any unused balance carries over to the next semester.

When it runs out: you get an email warning and printing stops until you top up. Move money from your CAMIPRO purse to myPrint in the web interface (on campus or via VPN). Transfers go one way only, and you cannot transfer print credit to a friend. A job that costs more than your balance is not printed at all.

Posters (approximate, standard paper): A2 CHF 2.75, A1 CHF 5.50, A0 CHF 11. A print in an unspecified format can be charged CHF 2, so always pick A4 or A3.

Avoid paying colour prices by accident: billing is per page, so one colour page in a 20-page file costs 19 black-and-white pages plus 1 colour page. Use the SecurePrint-BW queue for black-and-white jobs; "grayscale" options in some software (for example Adobe Acrobat) can still be billed as colour. You can also choose black and white when releasing the job at the printer.

How to print:

  • From your laptop: install the SecurePrint queues (EPFL has guides for Windows, Mac, Linux and smartphones). You must be on the EPFL network: Wi-Fi epfl or eduroam, or epfl-public plus VPN. See EPFL Wi-Fi.
  • From a Library computer: log in with GASPAR, print, then go to the printers after the Teaching Collection or in the Sciences and Techniques area.
  • Release the job: tap your CAMIPRO card on the printer, choose Secure Print, pick the job, print, then log out. Jobs you cancel at a multifunction printer are not charged.
  • Scan for free: "Scan and Send" then "Send to Myself" emails the file to you; "Scan and Store" saves to a USB stick. The Library also has free book scanners (up to 150 mm thick, A2 size) and a high-resolution scanner in the basement.
  • Printers elsewhere: EPFL lists 223 printers for staff and students on the campus map. Servers can be down briefly between 02:00 and 03:00 (Sundays 02:30 to 05:00).
  • Binding, thick paper, posters, mailing: the reprography centre on level 0 of the BP building; myPrint credit cannot be used there.

Problems: IT Service Desk 1234 (from outside +41 21 693 1234), 1234@epfl.ch, MA A0 392, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00. EPFL says the allowance is partly meant to absorb printing errors, so it does not handle claims under CHF 10 unless the accounting is clearly wrong.

Leaving EPFL: you can get back money you loaded yourself during your last semester (not the allowance), at the latest 1 month after your account closes, by emailing myPrint@epfl.ch. It goes back to your CAMIPRO purse; you can then collect it via AGEPoly, or by bank transfer if it is over CHF 20. See also Leaving and deregistering.

To verify: the myPrint FAQ (Library-computer section, under “For EPFL students and staff members”) still says the colour Canon after the Teaching Collection charges CHF 0.50 per sheet if you forget SecurePrint-BW, while the student prices page lists colour A4 at CHF 0.10 (and the same FAQ’s own “How much does an impression cost?” table matches that). myPrint Terms say EPFL rates are the same on every printer and that visitor rates are a little higher; visitor A4 colour duplex is 50 cent on the guest prices page. Both figures are still live on official myPrint pages (re-checked 5 Oct 2026; FAQ not updated). Check the price shown at the printer before you release a colour job.
