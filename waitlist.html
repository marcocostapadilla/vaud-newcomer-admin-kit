Optional: short human consult (waitlist)

Stuck on Vaud / EPFL admin? We are testing interest in a short human consult.

The free Vaud Newcomer Admin Kit covers the official steps. If you would like someone to walk through your situation with you (permit timing, insurance path, bank/documents checklist), we are measuring whether a short 1:1 consult would be useful. Nothing is for sale yet, and there is no charge to join the waitlist.

What you would get (when / if we open bookings)

  • A short 1:1 session focused on your paperwork path
  • Pointers back to the official sources in the kit
  • Not legal, tax, or immigration advice: orientation only

What this is not

  • Not a substitute for your commune, SEM, EPFL Student Services, or a lawyer
  • Not a promise of a permit, exemption, or bank account
  • Not open for payment. If bookings ever open, the price is shown clearly before anyone pays.

How to join

The waitlist sign-up is not open on this page yet. It will be added here when the site goes public. Until then, use the free kit: Start here · Glossary · First-year timeline.

Unofficial student orientation. Always confirm on the linked official pages. Joining the waitlist will not create a contract and will not authorise any charge.
