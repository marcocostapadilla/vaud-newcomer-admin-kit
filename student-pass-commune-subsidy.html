Student public-transport commune subsidies (Mobilis / tl)

Last checked: 5 Oct 2026. Unofficial student guide, not EPFL or government advice: confirm on the linked official pages before you act. Unfamiliar abbreviation? See the Glossary.

Companion to Getting around. No Marco asks. Prefer commas/parentheses over em dashes.

Why this page

EPFL’s public-transport page tells students that some communes subsidize season tickets, naming Lausanne, Epalinges, Ecublens and Chavannes-près-Renens, and asking you to contact your municipality. Half-Fare / GA / regional vouchers EPFL describes under “staff” are not the ordinary student path.

Source: EPFL, Public transportation

Day-1 buy/advice desk on campus: tl sales point, Maison de la mobilité (BI A0 432), Mon-Fri 9:00-13:00 / 13:30-17:00 (EPFL same page). Mobilis prices: mobilis-vaud.ch (re-check live).

Before you apply (any commune)

  • Be registered as a resident of that commune (Contrôle des habitants), not only “studying at EPFL.”
  • Buy the pass type the commune’s scheme covers (often annual Mobilis / FlexiAbo with named zones). Base fares change; use official Mobilis / tl pages.
  • Keep SwissPass + purchase receipt + enrolment proof if the form asks for them.
  • EPFL staff Half-Fare / GA / myCAMIPRO regional vouchers: staff rules (typically ≥50% contract ≥12 months). Ordinary Bachelor/Master students: use commune schemes + youth Mobilis tariffs, not those staff vouchers.

Lausanne

Hub: Rabais tl (distribution de bons)
Background: Subventions transports publics

Who (online bon request page): principal residence in Lausanne + AVS number + one of the listed categories, including jeune en formation (20-24 ans).

What Lausanne describes for jeunes en formation: 50% reduction on an annual Mobilis zones 11-12, with a CHF 40 participation (émolument). Example table on the subventions page (24-year-old in training) shows how that nets against the list price; re-check the live table before you budget.

Apply: online from the Rabais tl page; Lausanne states processing in principle 10 working days, then the bon by post.

Under 20 / school transport schemes: separate “transports scolaires” rules (often automatic attestation by post). University students 20-24: use the Rabais tl path above.

Renens

Hub: Subventions pour transports publics

Renens states you normally do nothing: bons are mailed once a year to eligible residents.

Annual Mobilis or FlexiAbo (must include zone 12):

  • CHF 150 for young people 16-24
  • CHF 150 for seniors at AVS reference age
  • CHF 250 for PC AVS/AI beneficiaries

Young people 16-24 receive the bon in June; seniors / PC in February. Arrivals mid-year: wait for the next annual mailing (Renens wording).

Ecublens

Hub: Subventions transports publics

Two different tracks (do not mix them up):

  1. Jeunes après scolarité obligatoire (cohésion sociale): commune news (Aug 2026) describes help for residents up to the year they turn 20, in gymnase / OPTI / transition / first unpaid CFC-type training / SEMO, for annual passes capped at a Mobilis 2-zone cost. To verify: whether your EPFL Bachelor/Master status and age fit that track; contact Service de la cohésion sociale et du logement (Place du Motty 4, 021 695 33 80 per that news item).
  2. Fonds développement durable form n° 10 “transports publics” (PDF état 20.8.2026, also listed under Subventions communales): inhabitant amounts include Mobilis annuel Fr. 200.- , demi-tarif Jeunes 16-25 Fr. 50.- , demi-tarif Adultes Fr. 100.-, AG Fr. 300.-; typically once every 5 years; SwissPass + purchase receipt; apply within 6 months of purchase; not cumulative with certain other communal subsidies (Passeport vélo exception). Open the live PDF before applying.

Chavannes-près-Renens

Hub: Subventions communales (online form + Conditions d’octroi pour subventions de transports publics)

Live conditions PDF (Conditions_octroi_subvention_TP.pdf; PDF metadata Mar 2024; hub page updated 17 Aug 2026). Flat CHF for inhabitants (one request per person, non-renewable, non-cumulative; apply within 6 months of purchase; invoice + proof of payment in the buyer’s name; for Jeunes passes, also a copy of the pass):

  • Half-Fare Jeunes 16-25: CHF 50
  • Half-Fare Adultes: CHF 100
  • Mobilis annuel: CHF 200
  • Abonnement Général: CHF 100
  • AG Famille: 20% of cost, max CHF 100

Scheme budget note in that PDF: up to CHF 2'500/month for this measure (max CHF 30'000/year total); once exhausted, no further awards that year. Contact on PDF/page: Service des Travaux (travaux@chavannes.ch / 021 633 33 60).

Also: Transports publics (network pointers + link to subventions).

Epalinges

EPFL lists Epalinges. Official entry: Subventions FEED (directive + forms; mobilité measures under FEED).

Directive FEED 2026 (PDF, in force from 18 May 2026; mobilité annual envelope CHF 200'000 indicative). Student-relevant Mobilis / FlexiAbo rows (annual nominative pass; 1-year wait before a second request; mobility form: apply within 6 months of purchase, with training proof where required):

  • Mobilis Jeune (en formation) ages 20-25: 40%, cap CHF 400
  • Mobilis Jeune (en emploi) 20-25: 20%, cap CHF 250
  • Mobilis Adulte (en formation) ages 25-40: 40%, cap CHF 400
  • Mobilis Adulte 25-65: 20%, cap CHF 250
  • FlexiAbo 100 Jeune 20-25: 40%, cap CHF 200
  • FlexiAbo 100 Adulte 25-65: 20%, cap CHF 150

Other AG / Demi-tarif PLUS / Interrail rows exist; open the Directive for the full table. Form: Mobilité durable 2026. Contact: energie@epalinges.ch / 021 785 61 65 (page).

To verify: whether your EPFL Bachelor/Master status fits « en formation » outside ages 20-25 (Jeune) or 25-40 (Adulte), or if you are under 20 (this Jeune-en-formation Mobilis measure starts at 20).

What this page does not do

  • It does not replace Mobilis youth tariffs or Half-Fare bought as a private customer on sbb.ch / tl.
  • It does not invent staff EPFL Half-Fare eligibility for students without an EPFL employment contract that meets LEX / Mobility staff rules.
  • It does not invent Mobilis list prices (base fares). Commune % / CHF above come from live conditions / Directive PDFs; re-check those PDFs before you budget.
